About

Designer, builder, and teacher.

I've worked in UX and technology since 2015, moving between research, design, engineering, and program leadership. That mix shapes how I work: I talk to people first, design the system, then build it so others can feel it for real.

How I work

  • Start with people. Interviews, observation, and usability testing come before pixels.
  • Design the system, not just the screen. Patterns, content rules, and components that whole teams can reuse.
  • Prototype in code. I build in SwiftUI so ideas can be tested on real devices, including haptics on Apple Watch.
  • Bring everyone along. I've led cross-functional teams across the US, EMEA, and Asia, and I love turning complex work into a clear story.
